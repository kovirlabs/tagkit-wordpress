A year ago I launched a small tool that turns CSV exports into readable reports. Today it has 340 paying customers, two of whom I've met in person. Here is what mattered, and what didn't.

Charge from day one

My free beta taught me almost nothing. The week I added a price, people started telling me what was missing.

Free users give you compliments. Paying users give you feedback.

A friend, far too late

Boring technology wins

The whole app is one server, one database, and a cron job. The deploy script fits on a postcard:

git pull
npm ci --omit=dev
systemctl restart reports
